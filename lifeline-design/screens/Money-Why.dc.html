Why fix for 5 years?

Your deal ends on 31 March 2027. After that you move to your lender's standard rate. It is usually much higher.
Today a 5-year fix costs less than a 2-year fix. That saves about £16 a month.
In 2023, people on short fixes saw payments jump from £695 to £1,150. A long fix protects you from a jump like that.
