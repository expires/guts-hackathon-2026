# ADR 0052: Play solo and Play with friends

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: accepted. Source: DIRECTION_V2.md section 7. Amends ADR 0044.

- Play opens with "Play solo" and "Play with friends".
- Solo is Life: student start paths (university, apprenticeship, job at 18, gap year, master's) and student profiles; banker, plumber, retired and nurse presets removed. Real earnings by occupation (Nomis/ASHE), real student loan repayment rules. "Play yourself" uses the user's data and real age. Student challenges: "Make your money last the term", "1 month safety fund", "Clear Klarna", "Lifetime ISA by 25".
- Quick Life: 2 minutes, one student path, ages 18 to 25 in 5 to 6 taps, 2 or 3 events with choices, summary card and habit score.
- Friends: lobby first (QR and short code, faces with ready tick), host picks Money Quiz, Same Decision or Same Life, options on one sheet. 2 players = duel, more = group. Names from the account. "Join" opens the scanner with "Type a code instead".
- Money Quiz: questions only from lessons and the facts table, each with its source; 15 s window; live ranking; Penny explains each answer in one line.
- Same Decision: same student money decision in the same piece of history; reveal of choices and outcomes (game money only).
- Circles: friends share habit scores only (savings rate, months of cover, spend stability, discretionary share, subscription creep, debt pressure), ratios 0 to 100 from the engine. Leaderboard ranks improvement vs own baseline. Weekly Penny recap, ratios only.
- Privacy rule: no £ amount, balance, income, transaction or merchant leaves the user's own view. Enforced by a contract test and V6.
