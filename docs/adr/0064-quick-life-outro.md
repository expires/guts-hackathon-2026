# ADR 0064: Quick Life demo outro

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: accepted. Amends ADR 0059 and 0061.

- Five premade Quick Life students (W3); the host picks one in the game sheet (GameOptions.quick_student_id).
- Room codes are 4 digits (0000-9999) for every room, easy to read aloud on stage. Guessing is limited by the existing join rate limits; rooms expire after 24 hours.
- Everyone plays the same events; the winner is decided by the result (INDEX and habit score); Penny announces the winner in one line (template, numbers from the result).
