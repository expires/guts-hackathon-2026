# ADR 0062: Ten sample students

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: accepted. Extends ADR 0047.

- Ten judge-facing sample students (plus the 4 team profiles): full maintenance loan in England (3 instalments); Scottish monthly loan plus bursary; scholarship plus bursary; wealthy parents; working student; commuter on a grant plus family help; mature student; international student (family money from abroad, FX transfers); final-year with internship pay; recent graduate on a first salary.
- Habits are mixed so income does not predict the habit score; a test checks the rank correlation between income and habit score is weak (|rho| < 0.3) and that at least one lower-income student beats a higher-income one.
- Every sample keeps the ADR 0047 rules: synthetic, 3 months, CSV and PDF, closing balances, labelled "Sample data".
