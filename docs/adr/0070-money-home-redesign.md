# ADR 0070: Money home redesign

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: accepted. Amends ADR 0066 (Money and You V2 blocks).

- The data pool sits under the Next Move. Order on Money home: Next Move card; weekly budget block; Spending this month (category bar and top categories); Bills, next 30 days; Subscriptions; Budgets and savings goals; one compact line "In your account £X · Net worth £Y" (tap for details); Guess my spend.
- The two big balance tiles are removed. "See all" stays for the full detail screen.
- Categories at £0 are hidden. Merchant rules for common UK student merchants keep "Other" under 15% of spending for every sample student.
- Every sample student has at least one budget and one savings goal that fit their story.
- The greeting follows local time: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and "Hi" late at night. Translated.
- The Next Move card always shows Test it when the move is testable.
- "No action needed" may appear only as a runner-up. Every sample student gets an active top move: when a real action applies, it ranks first. Student trade-offs fit students (no "less money invested" unless they invest).
