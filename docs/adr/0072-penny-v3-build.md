# ADR 0072: Penny V3 build (reseed, forecast Next Move, cut)

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: accepted. Source of truth: `PENNY_V3_BUILD.md` (repo root). It replaces the seed parts of DIRECTION_V2.md and wins where they disagree. Supersedes the Next Move card of ADR 0046 and ADR 0070, Guess my spend (ADR 0017) as a feature, and the Learn locks of ADR 0068.

- **Reseed (A):** the 10 sample students keep their names and stories; every number is rebuilt. Each income, rent and price figure has a source or is marked "example within <source> range" (`docs/seed_sources.md` and the sample metadata). Statements run from 1 July 2026 to the day before import. Town and region come from the card. No Monzo. Spending is calibrated to the National Student Money Survey 2026 per university (±15% in September). Research is limited to the figures in A2 and A3.
- **Forecast Next Move (B):** the decision is "Will my money last until my next money arrives, and how much can I spend each week?". Deterministic engine maths (B2): money now, bills before next money, a buffer (0, 5, 10, 15, 20%, default 10%), days to cover, per day and per week, usual spending over 4 weeks, on track or running short, spare or run-out date, CPI adjustment for gaps over 6 weeks, a 2022 stress test and one suggestion. Contract: `lifeline_contracts/forecast.py`, `GET /v1/forecast`, `PUT /v1/forecast/settings`, `GET /v1/finance/spending/{category}`, `SpendingResponse.months`, chat screen `forecast`.
- **Screens (B3):** Money home forecast card, Where your money goes, The maths, Detailed breakdown (adds up exactly), All spending with a month switch, Category. Design: the canvas at https://claude.ai/artifact/8epSoKzB7psGvgnnH7h5kn.
- **Removed (B4, F):** the old move card, Test it, Why?, Do this, plan line, weekly budget block, balance tiles, Guess my spend, every Mock or Sample data tag (one honesty line instead), the full Life game, Challenges, Same Decision, old Concepts, Collection, Debug menu and Playground in Release. Entry points hide behind one flag; code and tests stay. Play = Play with friends (Quick Life first) + Learn.
- **Onboarding (C), Penny (D), Learn (E), other fixes (G):** as listed in PENNY_V3_BUILD.md. Learn has no locks.
- **Order and rules (H):** location bug, reseed, forecast engine and screens, Try Penny verifier and double bubble, removals and cut, then onboarding, Penny, Learn. Small commits to the wip branch; one verifier run before 09:30; then merge to main.
