# ADR 0049: Student onboarding and judge mode

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: accepted. Source: DIRECTION_V2.md section 4.

- Flow: welcome (3 pages), sign in, "Connect your money" (Use a sample statement: 4 student cards; Upload a statement; Enter your numbers), live import progress (real pipeline), Next Move reveal, 3 tips.
- "Enter your numbers": student or working; age; postcode; housing (rent, with family, own, mortgage); income sources (multi-select, amount and schedule each; maintenance loan in 3 instalments in England, Wales and NI, monthly in Scotland); student loan plan or none; regular costs; savings; debts (card, overdraft, BNPL).
- Judge mode: You tab "New player" makes a fresh demo account and starts onboarding; "Reset demo" clears demo accounts and lobby state.
- Monzo and Trading 212 buttons are removed.
